Who leads, and exactly why

Capability leaders — Shadow, Sanctuary, Sharpa. Shadow's Dexterous Hand is still the dexterity ceiling (20 actuated DoF, 24 joints, ~$100–150k); Sanctuary's hydraulic Phoenix hand (~21 DoF, 5 mN micro-barometer tactile) and Sharpa's SharpaWave (22 DoF, >1,000 taxels per fingertip at 0.005 N) sit right beside it. Tactile leadership is concentrated in specialists (Sharpa, PaXini, Daimon, Sanctuary) while the frontier OEMs — Tesla, 1X, XPeng — disclose little about sensing. Strength is its own game: Robot Era's XHAND1 (80 N grip, 24 kg payload, fully actuated direct-drive) and BrainCo's Revo2 (50 N at 383 g — best strength-to-weight on the board).

Market leaders — LinkerBot, Inspire, Unitree. LinkerBot ships ~5,000 hands/month (scaling to 10,000) with a $920 entry model and claims 80% of global high-DoF volume; Inspire was first to 10,000 hands in a year and is the default third-party hand on Chinese humanoids; Unitree sells the Dex5-1 standalone at ~$5–8k on top of 5,000+ humanoids shipped in 2025, profitably. The Chinese price collapse — $150k-class imports to sub-$1,000 domestic hands in about three years — is the single most important market fact; hands are 15–20% of humanoid BOM, and whoever owns the cost curve owns the socket.

The convergence trade. Tesla, 1X, Figure, Sharpa, and XPeng have all converged on the same architecture: tendon-driven, ~22 actuated DoF, motors relocated to the forearm, tactile fingertips. Tesla and 1X are tooling for 10k+ unit volumes in 2026–27; Figure trails on DoF (16) but leads OEMs on published tactile sensitivity (3 g). Boston Dynamics and Agility deliberately went the other way — low-DoF grippers — betting factory tasks don't need 22 DoF yet.

The mispriced cells. High capability + distressed market: Sanctuary AI (best Western hand tech, restructured 2025, new CEO Jun 2026; Magna MGA is investor + strategic partner — an M&A candidate, not a scale player). High market + public ticker with hidden hand content: Zhaowei 003021.SZ (17-DoF direct-drive hand plus actuator content across 10+ humanoid programs) and fellow A-shares Leisai 002979.SZ and Jiangsu Leili 300660.SZ. The cleanest direct listing event ahead is Unitree's STAR-Market IPO (approved Jul 3 2026, ~$6.2B, ticker pending).
