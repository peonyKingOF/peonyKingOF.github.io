RoboSense Technology (2498.HK): Who Buys Its Sensors and Robot Components

Customer and supply-relationship map, 2021 – mid-2026 · Compiled July 5, 2026 · Sources: HKEX filings (IPO prospectus, annual/interim/quarterly results), company press releases, and third-party trade press and analyst commentary. Links inline in every cell.

Note on filings: RoboSense is listed in Hong Kong, not the US, so it has no SEC filings. The primary-source equivalents used here are its HKEX disclosures: the IPO prospectus (Dec 2023), FY2023 annual report, FY2025 annual report (with FY2024 comparatives), H1 2025 interim results, and the Q3 2025 unaudited announcement. Filings anonymize most customers ("a leading Chinese NEV maker"); names below come from company press releases and credible trade press, with confidence flagged.

Executive summary

RoboSense sells LiDAR and, increasingly, other robot components into three buckets. (1) Automotive ADAS is still the largest by revenue (RMB 1.11B of RMB 1.94B in FY2025): 167+ vehicle-model design wins from 35 automakers, anchored by an effectively exclusive BYD relationship, Geely/Zeekr, and a 2025–26 sweep of Sino-foreign JV brands (FAW Toyota >1.5M cumulative units ordered, Dongfeng Nissan ~1M), with a >9M-unit ADAS backlog entering 2026. (2) Robotics is the growth engine — shipments rose from 24,400 units (FY2024) to ~303,000 (FY2025, +1,142%) to 185,500 in Q1 2026 alone, when robotics passed ADAS in units for the first time. The volume driver is robotic lawnmowers (Mammotion's record 1.2M-unit order, Segway Navimow, Roborock), plus unmanned delivery (>90% of leading Chinese players plus Coco Robotics in the US) and robotaxis (exclusive win for Baidu's next-gen Apollo Go; WeRide GXR). (3) Humanoids are small in units but strategically central: ~50 humanoid/quadruped partners including Unitree, Agibot, Galbot and EngineAI, the "Qinglong" open-source humanoid (recipient of RoboSense's 1-millionth LiDAR), and a first large-scale Active Camera order from an unnamed leading European humanoid maker (2026). Beyond LiDAR, RoboSense now sells "incremental components": the Active Camera (AC1/AC2) fused sensor, Papert dexterous hands, force sensors, linear actuators and robot domain controllers.
